# Series Map

The visual curriculum follows a four-movement pilgrimage from **observation** to **immersion** to **discernment** to **transformation**.

## Movement I — Entering the World of the Text (Pages 1–11)

The reader learns to slow down, cross the threshold, and ask the world-opening questions **WHO, WHAT, WHERE, WHEN, and WHY**. The movement culminates in textual weather, sacred disorientation, and symbolic pressure.

## Movement II — Training the Interpretive Imagination (Pages 12–21)

The reader learns to hear voice and silence, feel accumulated repetition, recognize patterns and echoes, follow symbolic ecologies, track movement and turning points, hold contrasts, notice irony, cross thresholds, perceive hidden architecture, and finally read the whole field.

## Movement III — Reading the Human Condition (Pages 22–31)

The reader learns to recognize false worlds, power, empire, idolatry, fear, blindness, grief, desire, communal wounds, pressured hope, the human heart, and the revelation of God and humanity.

## Movement IV — Reading as Transformation (Pages 32–40)

The reader is located by the text, confronted in resistance, invited from information into formation, taught faithful reading postures, led into prayer and communal discernment, equipped with world-opening questions, sustained in lifelong practice, and sent back into life with transformed perception.
