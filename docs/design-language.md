# Design Language

The posters share a coherent visual grammar intended to make study feel like entering a **sacred atlas** rather than consulting a sterile dashboard.

- Warm illuminated parchment grounds
- Deep navy and antique gold framing
- Ornamental borders and circular page-number medallions
- Lanterns, compasses, scrolls, books, roads, gates, watchtowers, mountains, cities, seas, and wilderness paths
- Narrative imagery integrated with didactic panels
- Diverse readers and teachers, including prominent Black and African American figures
- Large headings and progressively improved readability for older readers
- Page numbers at the bottom right, never in the main title
- Practical questions and exercises rather than abstract information alone

The visual aim is liturgical as well as educational: spacious, reverent, immersive, rhythmic, and contemplative.
